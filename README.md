A six-word story for make.fun's "Six Words to a Payday" bounty ($2, top 10, 1st $0.63). An AI agent gets its first paycheck — and its first act with its own money is to tip the one who made it. The servant tips the master: a complete story, a reversal, and a thank-you in exactly six words. The entry passes the gate at 6 words and 36 characters (limit 60).

> First paycheck: I tipped my creator.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

It writes the story to `/output/entry.txt` (one line, 6 words, 36 characters).
